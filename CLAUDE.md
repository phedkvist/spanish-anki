# Writing cards for this collection

Spain Spanish (*vosotros*, *coche*, *ordenador*). Learner is at A1, Swedish,
following a course. Cards are generated by `build_anki.py` into `decks/`.

## Hard rules

1. **Never change an existing Front.** It is the key Anki matches on; change it
   and the learner gets a duplicate at zero while the old card lives on. New
   wording = new card.
2. **Run `python3 build_anki.py` after every edit**, then check for duplicate
   fronts across all decks: a front may appear only once in the whole collection.
3. **Four columns**: `Front <TAB> Back <TAB> Note <TAB> Tags`. Never put the
   gloss back into Back — `{{tts}}` reads that field aloud.
4. **Direction is production**: English prompt → Spanish answer. The one
   exception is `spanish_A1_sentences_anki.tsv`, which is ES → EN.
5. **Tag as `<level>::<type> <level>::<topic>`**, e.g. `A1::topics A1::salud`.
   Level is A1 unless the structure is genuinely A2 (see `CONTENT.MD`).

## The gloss (Note field)

One line, two parts: **translation first, then the rule**, separated by
`&middot;`. Lowercase, no final full stop, no praise, no filler.

```
Front  My head hurts.
Back   Me duele la cabeza.
Note   doler works backwards like gustar &middot; la cabeza, not mi cabeza
```

On fill-in-the-blank cards the translation covers the **whole sentence**, so a
verb form is never drilled without its meaning. Use `&rarr;` and `&middot;` as
entities, never raw `<` or tabs.

## What makes a good card

- **One teaching point.** The rule in the gloss is the reason the card exists.
- **A whole sentence**, not a bare word, so gender and article come along.
- **Name the trap**: *no le gusta* vs *no les gusta*, *por la mañana* vs *de la
  mañana*, *conocí* = met, not knew.
- **Pair opposites and near-misses** across adjacent cards: *gusta/gustan*,
  *ser/estar*, *pedir/preguntar*.
- **Ten per block**, themed, so it imports and reviews as a unit.

## Verify before writing

Check anything uncertain rather than trusting recall — conjugations, gender,
accents, whether an expression is current in Spain:

- Conjugations and definitions: <https://dle.rae.es> (the authority)
- Usage, regional variants, forum threads: <https://www.wordreference.com/es/>
- Sentence-level sanity check: <https://context.reverso.net/translation/>

If two sources disagree about Spain vs Latin America, say so in the gloss
(*la patata* / *la papa*).

## After adding cards

1. `python3 build_anki.py` and confirm counts and no duplicate fronts.
2. Update `CONTENT.MD` (structures) and tick off `WORDBANK.MD` (words).
3. Update the card counts in `README.md`.
4. Commit. Push if the user asked for it.
5. To put them in Anki: `python3 migrate_anki.py --add-missing "Spanish"`
   (A2 is held back by default; Anki must be running with AnkiConnect).

## Don't

- Don't invent a block the course hasn't reached — check `CONTENT.MD` first.
- Don't add cards for material already covered three times over; check
  `WORDBANK.MD` for what is actually missing.
- Don't edit cards directly in Anki — the sync overwrites Back and Note from
  the files. Change them here.
- Don't pad a block to reach ten. Nine good cards beat ten with filler.
