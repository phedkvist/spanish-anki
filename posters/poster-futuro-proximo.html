in the near future — part 1 of 2:
The whole tense in one rule
ir + a + infinitive
This is how people actually talk about plans — far more common in speech than the futuro simple. You only ever conjugate ir; the second verb stays in the infinitive and never changes.
Conjugate only ir — then bolt on any infinitive
haber is impersonal here: va a haber = there is going to be — always singular, even with plurals.
Object pronouns may sit either side: te voy a decir and voy a decirte are both correct.
