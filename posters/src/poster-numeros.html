Los Números

twenty-five words to learn, and a rule for the other seventy-five

1. Three zones only the first two have to be memorised

0 – 15
Sixteen separate words. No pattern, no shortcut — cero to quince.
16 – 29
Written as one word: dieciséis, veintiuno. Four carry an accent — marked in orange below.
31 – 99
Three words, joined by y: treinta y uno. Never one word, never without the y.
The join only exists from 31 up. 16–29 contract (dieci·séis, veinti·dós) and 31–99 do not (treinta y seis). There is no veinte y uno and no treintaiuno.

3. First to tenth after that Spanish gives up and uses the ordinary numbers

1ºprimero3ºtercero5ºquinto7ºséptimo9ºnoveno
2ºsegundo4ºcuarto6ºsexto8ºoctavo10ºdécimo
primero → primer and tercero → tercer before a masculine noun, exactly like uno → un:
el primer piso · el tercer día · but la primera vez, la tercera puerta — the feminine keeps its ending
Past tenth, nobody bothers: el piso veinte, el siglo veintiuno — plain numbers, and they follow the noun.

4. uno is the one that changes it agrees with whatever it counts

  • un euro — uno → un before a masculine noun
  • una hora — and una before a feminine one
  • veintiún días — veintiuno loses its o and gains an accent
  • treinta y un libros · treinta y una chicas
  • Somos cuatro en casa. — we are four, no word for of us
  • Tiene veinte años. — age is tener, never ser
  • El resultado es dos a cero. — scores take a
  • una docena de huevos — a quantity takes de
Count the memory load honestly: 0–15, the eight tens, and cien. That is twenty-five words. Everything from 31 to 99 is built, not learned.

5. The hundreds three of the nine are irregular — those are the ones to drill

100cien / ciento
200doscientos
300trescientos
400cuatrocientos
500quinientosirregular — not cincocientos
600seiscientos
700setecientosirregular — not sietecientos
800ochocientos
900novecientosirregular — not nuevecientos
cien is exactly 100 · ciento starts 101–199
cien euros · cien páginas — but ciento veinte personas

and there is no y after the hundred:
ciento veinte (not ciento y veinte)
doscientos treinta y cinco — the y belongs to 31–99, nowhere else
The hundreds agree in gender, which the tens never do: doscientas habitaciones, quinientas páginas, trescientos euros. Only cien and mil stay flat.

6. The thousands one word, and it never moves

1.000mil
2.000dos mil
3.000tres mil
4.000cuatro mil
5.000cinco mil
6.000seis mil
7.000siete mil
8.000ocho mil
9.000nueve mil
10.000diez mil
  • mil, never un mil — unlike English one thousand
  • dos mil, never dos miles — it does not pluralise as a number
  • mil quinientos euros — 1.500, built straight through
  • trescientos mil — 300.000, the hundreds come first
  • un millón — this one does take un
  • tres millones de habitantes — and always de before the noun

7. About, almost, half you need these far more often than an exact figure

  • unos veinte · unas treinta personas — about twenty · this is what unos is really for
  • más o menos cien — a hundred or so
  • casi mil — nearly a thousand
  • alrededor de cincuenta — around fifty
  • más de / menos de veinte — more / fewer than · de, never que, before a number
  • la mitad de la clase — half, as a noun
  • medio kilo · media hora — half, as an adjective, with no article
  • un tercio · un cuarto — a third · a quarter
  • el doble · el triple — twice · three times as much
  • el veinte por ciento — un descuento del veinte por ciento

8. Spain writes them the other way round the dot and the comma swap jobs

1.500
A dot marks thousands. English writes 1,500.
3,5
A comma marks the decimal, read tres coma cinco. English writes 3.5.
9,50 €
The symbol goes after, with a space: nueve euros con cincuenta.

9. Reading them out loud the places numbers actually turn up

  • 2026 — dos mil veintiséis — years are read straight, not in pairs
  • 1995 — mil novecientos noventa y cinco
  • el 15 de mayo de 2010 — plain numbers in dates, never quinceº
  • Son 9,50 €. — nueve con cincuenta at the till
  • el cuarto piso — ordinals only to about tenth, then plain: el piso veinte
  • la habitación 200 — la doscientos, keeping the article
The whole of 100–10.000 is three irregular words — quinientos, setecientos, novecientos — plus cien/ciento and the fact that mil never changes. Drill those five things and the rest assembles itself.
